Adult Lunchtime Hockey

Every Monday & Friday, 11:45 AM – 1:15 PM • Starting Sept 11th!

Take a break from the workday and get a great skate in at Wings Arena with our Adult Lunchtime Hockey —co-ed hockey open to players 18 and up, with all skill levels welcome in a fun environment. Drop in or register in advance, grab your full gear, and join us on the ice.

All sessions have a limited number of openings & these are filled based on the order of sign-up. Once the limit is reached, no additional skaters will be allowed in the session. You can register online in advance or at Wings Arena before the session.

Note: Full equipment is required.
