Adult Lunchtime Hockey

Mondays 11:45 AM – 1:15 PM • Fridays 11:40 AM – 1:10 PM • Now through Feb 2027!

Take a break from the workday and get a great skate in at Wings Arena with our Adult Lunchtime Hockey —co-ed hockey open to players 18 and up, with all skill levels welcome in a fun environment. Just drop in, grab your full gear, and join us on the ice.

All sessions have a limited number of openings & these are filled on a first-come, first-served basis. Once the limit is reached, no additional skaters will be allowed in the session.

Note: Full equipment is required.
